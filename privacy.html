Privacy Policy - rclone-backup

rclone-backup is a personal application used only by the owner of the connected Google account.

The application accesses Google Drive files in read-only mode for the sole purpose of creating local backup copies on the owner's personal computer.

Google Drive data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

OAuth credentials and backup files are stored locally on the owner's computer. No separate server operated by this application stores Google Drive data.

This application is not made available to other users.
